## en

- **The visual ruler measures the Crew in both themes (D18, D63), pending the
  owner's approval of each capture.** `check:visual` measured only 02 and 03,
  so every screen built since D41 had no reference and "0.00%" proved nothing
  about it. New references 05–12, each light and dark: the Crew with an
  approval and PR + CI, the empty Crew, the pinned plan with activity chips and
  documents, a loop at "ciclo 4" with its ruler open, the review card with
  earlier proposals, the Pronto tab, Nova with ↻ loop, and the ⌘K palette.
  Each is captured by the check itself, in fixture mode — a second recording
  for what the first cannot show without moving 02 and 03 — and reached by
  what `states.json` now says per state: the colour scheme, what the window
  finds in its storage, and the keys and clicks before the capture. Every
  state opens in its own browser context. 02, 03, the limit and pixelmatch are
  unchanged.
