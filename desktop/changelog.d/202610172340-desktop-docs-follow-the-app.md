## en

- **The desktop's documents say what the app does now** (D65). The README
  described the single-session window of the v2 design; it now describes the
  Crew as the main screen (D63), the grid as the alternative, the Agentes list
  (D64), the fixture states and the bundled Fragment Mono. AGENTS.md no longer
  justifies keeping `check:visual` out of CI with numbers measured against the
  handoff's references: the references are captures at 0.00% on macOS, and the
  distance on Linux has not been measured. The decisions are amended where the
  code moved past them — A3 is decided by D39, D31's approval keys are 1 to n,
  A5's settings open the theme (D35), D35's lime contrast table gives way to
  D52's, D52 lists the uses of the warn tone and the light terminal's ANSI
  tones with their contrast, the Crew's 8px corners are recorded in D63, Geist
  Mono as the Linux text fallback in D6 and D29, and the grid's "Na grade"
  marker in D30 —, the index gains D42, D44, D45 and D46 and says which file
  holds which range, and the loop proposal says it was built (D54–D56). New
  D65 records the name DCode Box, decided in #468 without a number, and why
  the top bar's brand stays "dcode": it names the tool, and the bar is in every
  measured reference.
